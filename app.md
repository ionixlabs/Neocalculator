## 1. Basic Information

* **App Name:** NeoCalculator
* **Description:** NeoCalculator is a modern high-performance calculator application built with Flutter designed to provide a seamless and visually stunning experience across Android iOS Windows macOS and Web. Featuring a sophisticated Neumorphic UI it combines a soft tactile aesthetic with powerful functionality. Whether you need to perform quick daily calculations or handle complex math expressions NeoCalculator delivers precision and style.
